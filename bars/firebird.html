Firebird

Vietnamese Craft Cocktails

Da Nang, Vietnam
“No syrup to hide behind.”
What I drank   /   01

Mango Mo Roi

Ingredients

Ruou Lang ETD · Enzyme Xoai · Ruou Xoai · Rieng Cordial · Tiopepe Sherry · Ancho Reyes · Chanh chua · Nuoc Muoi

The profile

Incredibly fresh mango on the nose. Light and refreshing. Dried mango garnish.

My thoughts

An amazing wine based cocktail. This drink uses classic Vietnamese rice & mango wine as its alcohol component, making for a cocktail incredibly easy to drink. The drink had a natural fruity sweetness.

For someone who likes their drinks more boozy, this probably isn't the one, but I felt it had lovely balance and was more complex than first thought.

For me personally, this is exactly what I want if I'm in the mood for a fruity drink. No syrup to hide behind. Makes me want to venture into more wine based cocktails.

My Verdict
8.5 / 10Taste
8.5 / 10Atmosphere
