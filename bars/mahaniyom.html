Mahaniyom

Bangkok, ThailandVisited July 2025
“Really a lot going on, but quite enjoyable.”
What I drank   /   01

Bee

Ingredients

Beeswax-Infused Scotch · Mezcal · Gin · Amaretto · Bee Pollen · Lime · Mead · Carbonated

The profile

A golden beer/honey color. Bubbles on top.

My thoughts

Such a unique drink. Truly ingredients I would never really think to put together. If I’m honest, seeing Scotch, Mezcal, Gin, and Amaretto listed, I had no idea how this drink was going to taste.

You first taste the citrus and fizziness, followed by the sweet and earthiness of the Scotch, finished with a nice smoky, almond aroma. Really a lot going on, but quite enjoyable.

“If ‘What the fuck’ was a cocktail.”
What I drank   /   02

Duck

Ingredients

Duck Fat–Washed Sherry Cask Whisky · Tequila · Brandy · Sweet Vermouth · Duck Blood Hoisin Liqueur · Lychee · Duck Egg · Honey · Duck Egg Spiced Meringue · Passionfruit Gel

The profile

Milky. Round ice. Elaborate garnish.

My thoughts

If “What the fuck” was a cocktail. I’ll start off by saying I do not prefer duck, but I had to order this drink purely out of curiosity. I’ve seen savory drinks before, but this was on a whole new level. Almost every ingredient had a duck component.

So I don’t think you’ll be surprised when I say my first sip tasted as if I just ordered Peking duck from my local Chinese spot. I mean, the accuracy and savory nature of this drink will knock you back in your seat.

For me, way too overwhelming. I love the innovation and the execution is stellar. If you like duck, this might be one of your favorite cocktails ever.

The Space

Just southeast of central Bangkok lives Mahaniyom. A second-floor bar above what looked to be a fantastic Thai restaurant. A very intimate, incredibly dark bar. A few bar stools, but mostly comfy chairs for couples.

I liked the neon sign above the bar. When I went, it was a quiet night, and I was able to chat with the friendly bartenders.

As you can see from the two drinks I ordered, their menu theme was creating drinks after animals. Everything you could look for in a bar, worth a visit.

My Verdict

Taste reflects what I drank. Atmosphere reflects the bar experience.
