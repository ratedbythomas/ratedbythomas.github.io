My thoughts

Ok! Please listen! You're probably like, "You gave it your seal of approval, but you only had one drink and don't remember it at all?"

No! Well... yes, but there's more to the story.

I have to admit that yes, I do not have much by way of content for this bar, visiting before my idea for this website. So please enjoy the one blurry picture I do have.

But here is the story of how I fell in love with this bar (also, The Space will explain some too).

After ordering my one unidentified drink, I got chatting with the gentleman to my right, who is an AI developer from Ukraine. He had quite the number of cocktails in front of him, mostly unfinished.

So I asked him why that was the case. He told me that he likes to order every drink from all the bars he goes to try them all. And shoot, if money isn't an issue, this is definitely the way to do it.

And lucky me for getting sat next to him, he offered for me to try all the drinks as well. So without spending any more money, I got sips of their whole menu.

At the time of me going, their theme was clarified cocktails. I want to say there were about 15 drinks on the menu, all clarified, and all delicious.

I spoke with the bartenders to understand their process, and I could tell just how much love and care went into each drink.

While I wish I had more to provide in terms of specific drinks and ingredients, just know these cocktails were absolutely fantastic.

The Space

This bar is so cool! It feels like you're in a rock... maybe even in a Star Wars set.

No right angles, and very earthy, natural materials. There's a few seats around the edge, but essentially the whole room is taken up by this massive bar top. The biggest I've seen. Just a giant square.

One well on the bar side and about 3 seats on each other side. It felt like a communal bar with everyone sharing that space.

Bar snacks were provided, which is always a plus.

It was an intimate, super unique and aesthetic bar. Clearly no-nonsense and just focused on producing top drinks.

I highly recommend it if you are visiting Lisbon.
