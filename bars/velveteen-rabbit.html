Velveteen Rabbit

Las Vegas, United StatesVisited November 2022
The Space

I really, really, really like this bar. And I’m unsure why I didn’t go more. Well, the answer is I didn’t want to drive and park in the Arts District, but that’s a little niche—for my Vegas locals.

The vibe is Dive Bar x Goth x Fairytale. For me, this is the best cocktail program in the entire city. The invention and creativity of their drinks and ingredients are truly fun to see. A bar team that takes tons of pride in their drinks and is obsessed with taking the time to hand-make everything they can.

For a city that is mostly known for overpriced and frozen drinks, Velveteen Rabbit is a breath of fresh air and should be right atop the list when visiting Las Vegas for any cocktail enthusiast.

My Verdict
8.2 / 10Taste
7.9 / 10Atmosphere

Taste reflects what I drank. Atmosphere reflects the bar experience.
