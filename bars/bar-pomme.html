Bar Pomme

Seoul, South KoreaVisited July 2025
“I’m really not sure how you could make a better Amaretto Sour.”
What I drank   /   01

Amaretto Sour

Ingredients

Milk-Washed Wild Turkey 101 Bourbon · Disaronno · Milky Oolong Tea · Korean Plum · Korean Pepper

The profile

Classic Amaretto Sour look with a Korean plum garnish.

My thoughts

Heck fricken’ yeah. I won’t stand for the shit talk that loving Amaretto Sours garners. The drink is objectively good, and no, I am not having that it is an old person drink. I will not let this drink be age-gatekept like they tried to do with Arnold Palmers.

If you love Amaretto Sours, this is a must-order. And if you don’t, give this one a try and I’ll wager the cost of the drink that it’ll change your mind.

The milk washing and the oolong tea gave the drink a richness and silky texture which amplified the Disaronno. The addition of bourbon to this drink did not change the balance of this classic; it simply provided a little bit more bite and a fullness which I really appreciated.

The Korean pepper, for me, is a fantastic choice to provide an ever-so-subtle spice to counteract the incredibly sweet nature of this drink. I learned about Korean plums on this trip, and man, am I a fan. An incredibly delicious fruit—I had Korean plum wine and Korean plum tea. A great flavor to add to this drink.

I’m really not sure how you could make a better Amaretto Sour. I was thoroughly impressed.

The Space

This bar flat-out rocks. As I mentioned in my Bar Cham review, it is their sister bar. This is like the quiet girl in high school who has a super popular sister that everyone loves. But the quiet sister is smart, kind, and fun in her own right. A girl you always had your eye out for, but teenage societal norms stopped you from talking to her.

What I’m trying to say is, you should have given her a chance. Good news for you is, you have a second chance here with Bar Pomme.

Merely steps away from Bar Cham, you will not be met with a wait, only with incredibly friendly bartenders and a relaxed atmosphere. The vibe is a traditional-style Korean bar/pub who dresses super nice. Their menu leans classic riffs, using primarily Korean spirits and flavors.

Do not overlook this bar, even if it may not have the accolades of the others.
